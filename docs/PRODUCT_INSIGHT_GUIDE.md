# Product Insight: Test Script for Tab 01

The brief says *"Use the product."* The five observations must come from your own session in Creator Studio, with screenshots or a screen recording as evidence. Nothing here is pre-filled. This is the route to walk so the observations connect to what the outreach promises.

Every outreach message promises **"three ready-to-post clips from your latest episode."** Your observations should test whether the product delivers that promise for these creators.

| # | Product area | What to do | What to record (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign-up / onboarding | Sign up from the homepage "Start free". Time it from click to first project. | Time taken, steps, any card or verification wall (screenshots) |
| 2 | Content Creation: clips from long video | Upload a 30–60 min podcast episode (use your own or one that is CC-licensed). | Processing time, how many clips it found, whether they are good enough to post as-is |
| 3 | Captions | Open a clip and check the word-by-word captions, styling and accuracy. | Caption errors per minute; whether fonts/colours can be changed |
| 4 | Transcript editing | Delete a sentence in the transcript and check the video cut. | Whether the cut is clean; any audio pop or desync |
| 5 | Content Publishing | Connect one platform (e.g. a test YouTube or Instagram account) and schedule a clip. | Which platforms connect, any errors, the time to schedule |
| (alt) | Content Insights | Ask for topic ideas in a niche (e.g. "business coaching podcast"). | Whether the ideas are specific and current |
| (alt) | Content Performance | The website says "rolling out now / coming soon". | Whether it is available in your account (date, time, screenshot) |

If something is unavailable, record it with the date, time and a screenshot. The brief says this counts, and you shouldn't try to work around it.

## Writing each row

- **What happened:** facts only ("uploaded a 47-min MP4; 11 clips returned in 6 min").
- **What worked or was difficult:** one strength or one friction point.
- **Why it matters for growth:** tie it to the outreach. For example: "the sample-clips offer in every message depends on this taking < 10 min per lead; at 6 min, one person can serve ~40 sample requests a day."

The three **opportunities** (tab 01, section B) are pre-filled from the lead data, because that is evidence the engine produced. Re-check them against what you saw in the product and edit if needed.
